Support Our Mission

Donations help DIRA Foundation carry out its objectives, including education and skills training, enterprise support, research, health awareness and community development. Under our constitution, all funds are applied to the foundation's lawful objectives.

What Support Can Go Toward

  • Digital literacy and skills training
  • Events and knowledge sharing
  • Youth and women's enterprise support
  • Research and innovation
  • Health awareness and WASH
  • Scholarships and bursaries

Volunteer Opportunities

Roles that support the foundation's outreach, health and wellbeing, enterprise and programme work

Field Workers

Community Outreach

Work directly with communities, groups and enterprises to carry out the foundation's activities on the ground.

Key Responsibilities

  • Carry out community outreach and awareness activities
  • Mobilise participants for events, trainings and surveys
  • Collect information and record attendance accurately
  • Support partner institutions and enterprises during field visits
  • Report observations and challenges to the project manager

Skills & Experience

  • Good knowledge of local communities in the Northwest Region
  • Fluency in English and, ideally, French or a local language
  • Willingness to travel within the region
  • Reliability and respect for confidentiality
  • Community, survey or outreach experience is an advantage
Apply Now

Communications Officer

Communications

Tell the story of the foundation's programmes and keep the public, partners and institutions informed.

Key Responsibilities

  • Write news items, event summaries and reports
  • Plan and publish content on the foundation's channels
  • Document events with photographs and short interviews, with consent
  • Prepare publicity for events and partner communications
  • Keep messaging accurate and consistent across materials

Skills & Experience

  • Strong written English; French is an advantage
  • Experience in communications, journalism or public relations
  • Basic photography or content-gathering skills
  • Respect for consent, privacy and accuracy
  • Availability agreed with the team
Apply Now

Psychologist

Health and Wellbeing

Provide psychological support and mental health awareness within the foundation's community programmes.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead awareness sessions on mental health and wellbeing
  • Offer psychosocial support or refer participants to suitable services
  • Advise the team on participant welfare and safeguarding
  • Train field workers in supportive ways of responding to distress
  • Keep all participant information confidential

Skills & Experience

  • Qualification in psychology and relevant registration where applicable
  • Experience working with young people, women or community groups
  • Knowledge of local support and referral services
  • Strong ethics and commitment to confidentiality
  • Availability agreed with the team
Apply Now

GBV Counsellor

Health and Wellbeing

Support survivors of gender-based violence and help communities work to prevent it.

Key Responsibilities

  • Provide confidential, survivor-centred counselling
  • Refer survivors safely to health, legal and protection services
  • Support awareness and prevention sessions in communities
  • Advise the team on handling disclosures safely and respectfully
  • Work with partner organisations and local services

Skills & Experience

  • Training and experience in GBV counselling or a related field
  • Understanding of survivor-centred and do-no-harm approaches
  • Knowledge of local referral services
  • Strict commitment to confidentiality and safeguarding
  • Availability agreed with the team
Apply Now

Fiscal Experts and Business Consultants

Enterprise Support

Advise small businesses and cooperatives on formalisation, finance and growth.

Key Responsibilities

  • Guide enterprises on registration and record-keeping
  • Explain taxes, compliance and basic accounting in plain terms
  • Support business planning and access to financial products
  • Deliver coaching and training sessions for entrepreneurs
  • Work with partner institutions that support small enterprises

Skills & Experience

  • Background in accounting, taxation, finance or business advisory
  • Experience working with small enterprises or cooperatives
  • Ability to explain technical topics clearly
  • Professional integrity and discretion
  • Availability agreed with the team
Apply Now

Project Managers

Programme Delivery

Plan and coordinate the foundation's programmes from preparation through to reporting.

Key Responsibilities

  • Plan activities, timelines and budgets with the team
  • Coordinate volunteers, partners and suppliers
  • Track progress, risks and spending
  • Prepare reports for partners and the Executive Committee
  • Support monitoring and evaluation of results

Skills & Experience

  • Experience managing projects, preferably in community, non-profit or enterprise settings
  • Strong organisation, budgeting and reporting skills
  • Ability to work with institutions and volunteers
  • Project management training is an advantage
  • Availability agreed with the team

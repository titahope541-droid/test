Partners and Institutional Collaboration

DIRA Foundation works with government institutions, community organisations and others in pursuit of its objectives.

Government and Regional Institutions

Collaboration with ministries, agencies and public institutions is one of the foundation's stated objectives. These are institutions the DIRA Foundation team has engaged with in the Northwest Region.

Government institution

MINRESI, North West Regional Delegation

The Regional Delegation of Scientific Research and Innovation for the North West Region. DIRA Foundation team members have been received at its offices. Its field relates to our objective of supporting youth-owned and women-owned enterprises.

Regional institution

North West Regional Assembly

Assemblée Régionale du Nord-Ouest. DIRA Foundation team members have visited the Assembly, in the region where the foundation's activities are based.

How Institutions Can Collaborate With Us

The constitution invites partnership with government ministries and agencies, traditional authorities, academic and research institutions, development finance institutions, civil society organisations and the private sector. Areas where we can work together include:

Education and digital inclusion

Digital literacy, skills training, bootcamps, scholarships and bursaries.

Enterprise support

Incubation, mentorship and capacity building for youth-owned and women-owned enterprises.

Research and innovation

Applied research across technology, agriculture, health, environment and construction.

Health and WASH

Healthcare campaigns, awareness raising and water, sanitation and hygiene projects.

Events and knowledge sharing

Conferences, exhibitions and hackathons, including DIRA Tech Fest.

Institutional membership

Organisations can be admitted as Institutional Members in a supporting, sponsoring or advisory capacity, without voting rights. Applications are made in writing to the Executive Committee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about collaborating with DIRA Foundation

Who can collaborate with DIRA Foundation? +
Government ministries and agencies, traditional authorities, academic and research institutions, development finance institutions, civil society organisations, private companies and individuals can all propose collaboration, provided it supports the foundation's objectives.
What kinds of collaboration are possible? +
Collaboration can cover education and digital literacy, support for youth-owned and women-owned enterprises, research, health and WASH initiatives, events and knowledge sharing, and sponsorship of programmes.
Can an organisation become a member? +
Yes. The constitution provides for Institutional Members: organisations admitted in a supporting, sponsoring or advisory capacity, without voting rights. Admission follows a written application to the Executive Committee.
How do I get in touch? +
Use the form on this page or the contact page. Your message is prepared as an email to the foundation, so please include your organisation's name and the area you are interested in.

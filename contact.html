Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about contacting DIRA Foundation

How quickly will you reply? +
We reply as soon as we can. Please include your organisation, if any, and the topic of your enquiry so that your message reaches the right person.
How can I arrange a meeting? +
Send us a message describing the purpose of the meeting and who would attend, and we will get back to you.
How can my organisation collaborate with the foundation? +
See the Partners page for the areas where we can work together and a short collaboration form.
How can I volunteer or become a member? +
Volunteer roles are listed on the Get Involved page. For membership, send us a message and we will explain how to apply to the Executive Committee.

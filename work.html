Programmes and Events

Programmes and events advancing innovation and community development in Bamenda and across the North West Region.

Event · Completed

DIRA Tech Fest 2026

The first edition of DIRA Tech Fest ran for three days in Bamenda under the theme "Dare to Dream: From Ideas to Products". It combined free skills training for young people, a public summit, a startup pitch competition and a business exhibition.

On the first two days, 108 secondary school students, university students and recent graduates trained across ten tracks, from web development and design to digital marketing, public speaking and cybersecurity basics. They then worked in ten mixed teams and each delivered a working product or prototype within 48 hours.

On the third day, five finalist teams pitched live to a hall of more than 750 people, and 45 businesses took part in an exhibition with free exhibition space.

  • Dates: 27 to 29 March 2026
  • Location: Bamenda, Northwest Region (LTC Hall for training, Cathedral Hall for the summit and exhibition)
  • Participants: Young people, local businesses and the public
  • Organised by: DIRA Foundation

Summit and institutions

A panel on the region's digital economy brought together delegates of three ministries (MINRESI, MINPROMALO and MINRESI) and the regional manager of NFC Bank. According to the report, the MINRESI delegate publicly pledged support for the exhibiting businesses.

After the event

Prizes totalling 70,000 XAF went to four teams, led by a waste management platform for Bamenda. Four of the ten teams, including finalists, are being mentored toward legal registration and growth, and a Bamenda tech startup coalition was seeded at the event.

Ongoing Programme

Programme · Ongoing

JERSI-NW 9th Edition: youth mobilisation and publicity

JERSI-NW is the North West Open Day of Excellence for Scientific Research and Innovation, organised by the Regional Delegation of the Ministry of Scientific Research and Innovation (MINRESI). The 9th edition is scheduled for 10 to 12 November 2026 at CRRI-NW, Up Station, Bamenda.

DIRA Foundation is supporting the Delegation, at no cost and as part of its community programme, as mobilisation and publicity partner. Its role is to mobilise young people through youth organisations and school and university networks, to design and distribute promotional materials credited to both MINRESI and DIRA Foundation, and to coordinate publicity on the ground in the two weeks before the event.

The collaboration builds on the Delegation's participation in the DIRA Tech Fest 2026 panel.

  • Partner institution: MINRESI, North West Regional Delegation
  • Dates of the event: 10 to 12 November 2026
  • Related objective: Research and innovation, and collaboration with public institutions

Planned Programme

Programme · Planned for 2027

DIRA Startup Incubator Program (DSIP)

DSIP is a programme owned by DIRA Foundation to help small businesses and cooperatives in the Northwest Region register, keep proper records and grow, while training people in business, finance, marketing and technology. Priority will go to women-led and youth-led enterprises.

Many enterprises in the region operate without registration or written records, which keeps them from banks, public buyers and investors. The programme responds in three stages:

  1. Identification. A readiness survey of 30 to 50 enterprises, cooperative credit unions and social-economy organisations across the seven Divisions. It is financed by DIRA SAS and carried out under a Memorandum of Understanding with the MINRESI Regional Delegation.
  2. Formalisation and training. Selected enterprises complete registration and record-keeping with a mentor and receive coaching through the Agency for the Promotion of Small and Medium-Sized Enterprises (APME). Five hundred trainees follow a common core and four expert-led tracks: business development, finance and banking, sales and brand, and technology and AI. Partner ministries brief participants on opportunities and compliance. This stage is planned for 20 July to 19 August 2027.
  3. Closing Tech Summit. Two days at Bamenda Congress Hall, on 20 and 21 August 2027, with an exhibition, a pitch competition, panels, a government address and recognition of the formalised enterprises and graduates.

What the programme aims to achieve

  • At least 30 enterprises registered and equipped with records, each with a written starting assessment and a mentor.
  • 500 people trained, with women making up at least 40 percent of participants.
  • A regional readiness index delivered to the MINRESI Regional Delegation at no cost to government.
  • Location: Bamenda and the seven Divisions of the Northwest Region
  • Programme cost: 100,000,000 XAF, including a protected prize pool of 10,000,000 XAF
  • Accountability: The foundation holds all funds in its own account, large payments need two authorising officers, an independent chartered accountant reviews the accounts, and partners receive quarterly reports and a full report within 30 days of the summit.

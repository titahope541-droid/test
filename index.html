Who We Are

DIRA Foundation exists to advance digital innovation, education, entrepreneurship, research and community development in Cameroon, so that individuals, institutions and communities can build, access and benefit from the digital and knowledge economy.

We are apolitical and non-religious, and we operate exclusively on a non-profit basis. DIRA Foundation is the philanthropic arm of DIRA Group, and it is a distinct non-profit association whose income and assets are used only for its own objectives.

Technology is one part of what we do. Alongside it, our constitution commits us to education, enterprise support, research, health awareness and collaboration with institutions.

Our Areas of Work

DIRA Foundation works across the following areas:

Digital literacy and skills

Digital literacy, technology education and skills training for young people, women and underserved communities.

Events and knowledge sharing

Conferences, exhibitions, hackathons and bootcamps that promote innovation, entrepreneurship and digital inclusion, including DIRA Tech Fest.

Youth and women's enterprise

Support for youth-owned and women-owned enterprises through incubation, mentorship, capacity building, seed grants and access to networks.

Research and innovation

Applied research in information technology and AI, agriculture, health and biomedical science, environmental science, and construction and materials science, shared for public benefit.

Health and WASH

Healthcare campaigns, awareness raising, water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) work, and other health and health-tech projects.

Scholarships and education support

Scholarships, bursaries, seed grants and endowment funds that support education, research and youth and community development.

Data and information security awareness

Awareness of data sovereignty, records digitisation and information systems security among institutions and the public.

Housing, real estate, transport and logistics

Awareness and good practice in the housing, real estate, and transport and logistics sectors.

Institutional collaboration

Partnerships with government ministries and agencies, traditional authorities, academic and research institutions, civil society and the private sector.

Terms of Use & Privacy

How this website works, what to expect when engaging with DIRA Foundation, and how information shared through the site is handled.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

DIRA Foundation is a non-profit association based in Bamenda, Cameroon. This page gives a practical overview of our website terms and privacy practices. It is not legal advice; please contact us if you need clarification.

Terms of Use

By using this website, you agree to use it lawfully and respectfully. These terms apply to the public pages, articles, programme information, volunteer application materials and online donation entry points made available here.

Information about our work

Website content is provided for general information about DIRA Foundation and its activities. Programme plans, dates, eligibility, budgets, targets and opportunities may change. Contact us to confirm current details before making decisions or commitments. Nothing on this site is professional, financial, legal or investment advice.

Applications, partnerships and donations

Applying to volunteer, expressing interest in a programme or proposing a partnership does not guarantee selection, funding, participation or any other outcome. Donations are voluntary and are used to advance the Foundation's lawful objectives. Online payments are processed by Fapshi under its own terms; contact Fapshi about payment processing and contact us promptly if a payment appears incorrect.

Content and acceptable use

Unless otherwise stated, the text and materials published by DIRA Foundation belong to the Foundation or are used with permission. Third-party names, marks, photographs and links may belong to their respective owners. Do not copy, alter, republish or use materials in a way that misrepresents their source or violates another person's rights.

Do not attempt to disrupt the site, access non-public systems, interfere with other users, submit unlawful or misleading material, or use the site to distribute malicious code. We may restrict access where necessary to protect the site, users or Foundation.

External services and availability

The site may link to external platforms, including Fapshi, social networks, email services and partner websites. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy practices; DIRA Foundation does not control them. We aim to keep the website available and accurate but cannot promise uninterrupted access or that every page will always be error-free.

Responsibility and governing law

To the extent permitted by applicable law, DIRA Foundation is not responsible for losses arising from reliance on general website information, temporary unavailability, or third-party websites and services. Nothing in these terms excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded. These terms are governed by the laws applicable in Cameroon, subject to any mandatory protections that apply to you.

Privacy Notice

We handle personal information with care and use it to communicate with people, coordinate Foundation activities, administer donations and maintain the website. We do not sell personal information.

Information you choose to provide

  • Contact enquiries: the name, email, organisation and message you enter in the contact form. The form opens your device's email application; your information is sent to us only if you choose to send that email.
  • Volunteer applications: the details you enter, which can include contact information, date of birth, gender, education, skills, availability, emergency contact and motivation. The application is prepared in your email application. It is not submitted to DIRA until you send the email. You attach any CV yourself.
  • Donations: the amount and optional email you enter are sent to Fapshi to create a checkout. Fapshi collects and processes payment details directly under its own privacy notice and terms.
  • Article administration: information submitted to the password-protected editor by authorised administrators, including article text and images. Published material is visible publicly. Article records are stored in the Foundation's configured database and images may be stored with its image-hosting provider.

Technical information and storage

Our hosting and infrastructure providers may process standard technical information such as IP address, request time, browser details and requested page in service logs and for security, diagnostics and operation of the site. The donation page temporarily stores a transaction reference and a signed status-check token in your browser's session storage so it can check the result after you return from checkout. The administrator area uses an essential signed, HttpOnly session cookie to keep authorised administrators signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers on the pages reviewed for this notice.

How information is used and shared

We use information to reply to enquiries, assess and coordinate volunteer applications, communicate about programmes and partnerships, reconcile donation transactions, publish articles, and protect and maintain the website. Information may be handled by service providers supporting email, hosting, database, image storage or payment services, and may be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect people, rights or the Foundation. Providers may process information in countries other than Cameroon.

Retention and your choices

We keep messages and application information for as long as needed for the relevant communication, programme administration, safeguarding, accountability or legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it when reasonably practicable. Published articles and associated records may be retained as part of the Foundation's public record. Technical logs and payment records may be retained by our service providers under their own policies and applicable requirements.

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have sent, request a correction or deletion where appropriate, or raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may retain information where required for legal, safeguarding or accountability reasons. Your rights depend on applicable law.

Children and sensitive information

Please do not include sensitive personal information about yourself or another person unless it is necessary for your enquiry or application. If you provide information about someone else, make sure you are authorised to do so. A parent or guardian should contact us before submitting information on behalf of a child.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the website or our practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with its revision date. For questions or privacy requests, email Foundation@diracmr.com.

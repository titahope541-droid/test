About DIRA Foundation

A non-profit association working for education, opportunity, research and community development in Cameroon.

Who We Are

DIRA Foundation is a Bamenda-based non-profit association. We work with communities, young people, entrepreneurs and institutions across Cameroon on education, digital skills and opportunities for local businesses.

By 2030, we aim to establish the DIRA Startup Incubator Program as a formal, continuing programme, build a Bamenda-based hub for technology businesses and cooperatives across the Northwest Region, and secure an endowment of more than XAF 10 billion to support the Foundation's long-term work.

The foundation is based in Bamenda, North West Region.

Our Mission and Objectives

The foundation's objectives include:

  1. Promote digital literacy, technology education and skills training among young people, women and underserved communities.
  2. Organise DIRA Tech Fest and other conferences, exhibitions, hackathons, bootcamps and knowledge-sharing events.
  3. Support youth-owned and women-owned enterprises through incubation, mentorship, capacity building, seed grants and access to markets and networks.
  4. Promote and support applied research and innovation in information technology and AI, agriculture, health and biomedical science, environmental science, and construction and materials science, and share the findings for public benefit.
  5. Promote awareness and good practice in the housing, real estate, and transport and logistics sectors.
  6. Promote data sovereignty, records digitisation and information systems security awareness.
  7. Establish and administer scholarships, bursaries, seed grants and endowment funds for education, research and youth and community development.
  8. Partner with government ministries and agencies, traditional authorities, academic and research institutions, development finance institutions, civil society and the private sector.
  9. Receive and manage donations, membership contributions, grants, sponsorships and endowments for these objectives.
  10. Carry out health-related projects, including healthcare campaigns, awareness raising and WASH.

Non-Profit Character and Legal Status

Exclusively non-profit

Our assets and income are dedicated to our objectives. No part of our net income or assets goes to any founder, member, officer or Executive Committee member, other than reasonable pay for services actually rendered or reimbursement of proper expenses.

Relationship with DIRA Group

DIRA Foundation is the philanthropic arm of DIRA Group. Our founder also founded DIRA Group and its commercial entities. The foundation is a separate non-profit association, and the commercial activities of DIRA Group are not foundation programmes.

How We Work

Our approach follows the principles written into our constitution.

Collaboration

We work with ministries and agencies, traditional authorities, academic and research institutions, civil society and the private sector.

Inclusion

Young people, women and underserved communities are central to our objectives, and at least one member of our Executive Committee must be a woman.

Public benefit

We share the findings of the research we support so that the wider community can benefit from it.

Accountability

We are accountable to our members and the communities we serve. We are committed to using our resources responsibly and being open about our activities and results.
